# Download page (index.html, ar.html)

Mode: Persuade.

- Audience: the general public, many Arabic speakers, mostly on the Android phone they'll install on.
- Job: understand what Manara does, trust it enough to sideload, install it, allow precise location.
- Primary action: Download for Android (the GitHub Release's latest `Manara.apk`).
- Content, in order: the lantern room with the download; what Manara detects; privacy; install
  help; About.
- About holds the team (SDP-G25, Qatar University, the supervisor and the four members) and must
  stay "hidden": collapsed at the foot of the page, opened on tap, never on the main stage.
- Language: opens in the phone's language (Arabic or English) and remembers the visitor's switch.
- The boats in the opening scene are an example scan, captioned as such; no real network names
  from the team's screenshots.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page opens on the app's own lantern room: Manara's beam sweeps a night sea and lights each network, drawn as a boat, with its verdict, one of them a fake copy. It refuses the category's phone mockup over a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: The app's night palette (navy #071222 and #0B1A2E, sea #0E2742, raised #1B2F4A, warm white, gold beam and button) and its daymark palette by day; cream and brick tower bands with navy portholes holding the buoy topmarks; railing rules; Fraunces titles, El Messiri Arabic, Aref Ruqaa wordmark.

STORY: The visitor watches a fake network get caught, learns Manara checks Wi-Fi before they join, trusts it because the work stays on the phone and verdicts are honest, downloads, and follows five install steps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top 55%: night sea, lighthouse at the leading edge, beam over four labelled boats (Home Secure, an open copy of Corniche Vulnerable beside a danger buoy, Café Guest Unknown, one far boat); language switch in the corner. Then the railing and the gallery card: lockup, one-line tagline, the amber Download for Android button, version and size.

FORM: Lantern room sweep, position 1 of 7, seed:29e83841e912549b. Signature interaction: one slow beam sweep lighting the boats in turn; nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
