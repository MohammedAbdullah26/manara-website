Check a Wi-Fi network before you trust it.

Every check runs on your phone.

Manara spots fake copies of networks and risky hotspots before you join them, and does every check on your phone.

On a computer? Open this page on your Android phone to install Manara.

Four answers, and never a guess

Every network in range gets a score from 0 to 100 before you connect, and one of four marks: the buoy shapes sailors read at sea.

  • Secure 75 to 100

    Nothing worrying in what Manara could check.

  • Moderate 40 to 74

    Something to be careful about, such as an open network or a hidden name.

  • Vulnerable below 40

    It may be fake or unsafe. Better not to join it.

  • Unknown too little checked

    Manara couldn't check enough, so it won't call the network safe.

What Manara catches

  • Fake copies of a network

    An open network using the name of one that has a password is flagged before anyone joins it, and the real one is marked too.

  • Copies that borrow the router's address

    When one name and address keep switching security or band, two devices are sharing it. From Android 11, Manara also notices when the radio hardware itself changes.

  • Hotspots dressed as a business

    A phone hotspot named like a hotel, café or airport is spotted from its address and the phone maker behind it.

  • A five-stage check of your connection

    Once you're connected, Manara checks how the network really behaves, and only calls it Secure when enough of the checks finished.

  • A traffic test with on-device AI

    An optional 30-second test watches how the traffic flows. The AI model runs on your phone, and it never decides a verdict on its own.

  • A radar to find the router

    Locate turns your screen into a radar that guides you toward the access point.

  • Warnings from phones nearby

    When you turn it on, Manara phones warn each other about networks that score below 40.

Your phone does the work

  • Scanning, scoring, the five-stage check and the AI model all run on the phone.
  • The traffic test counts packets and builds statistics about the traffic. It never stores what's inside the packets.
  • Nearby warnings, online reports and cloud backup stay off until you turn them on. Reports and backup need an account.
  • Saved networks are encrypted with a key that never leaves your phone's secure storage.

Install in five steps

Manara isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks before installing it.

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for the file to finish downloading.

  2. Open Manara.apk from the download notification, or from Downloads in your Files app.

  3. If Android says your browser can't install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.

  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

  5. Open Manara and choose Precise location. Android only gives Wi-Fi scan results to apps that have it.

"App not installed"? An older copy of Manara built on another computer is in the way. Uninstall it, then try again.
